About the Indian Army

The Indian Army is the land-based branch and the largest component of the Indian Armed Forces. With over 1.4 million active personnel, it is the world's second-largest standing army and has the world's largest volunteer army. The Indian Army's primary mission is to ensure national security and national unity, defend the nation from external aggression and internal threats, and maintain peace and security within its borders.

The Indian Army has a rich history of bravery and sacrifice. From the icy heights of Siachen to the deserts of Rajasthan, from the dense jungles of the northeast to the high-altitude battlegrounds of Ladakh, Indian Army soldiers stand guard 24/7, 365 days a year, in the most challenging conditions imaginable.

The motto of the Indian Army is "Service Before Self," and its soldiers live by this principle every day. Whether it's defending the nation's borders, providing disaster relief during natural calamities, or assisting in nation-building activities, the Indian Army is always at the forefront.

Core Values

Duty

To defend the nation and its interests with absolute commitment, regardless of the cost.

Honor

To uphold the highest standards of ethical conduct and moral integrity at all times.

Courage

To face challenges and adversity with physical and moral strength.

Sacrifice

To place the nation's security and welfare above personal comfort and safety.
